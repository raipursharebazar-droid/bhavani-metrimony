About Us

Running for 25 years, built entirely by women.

Bhavani Matrimony is one of Andhra Pradesh's most trusted matrimonial platforms — a one-stop place for families to find the right match.

Our Story

Founded 25 years ago, Bhavani Matrimony set out to be a dependable, affordable matrimonial service for families across Andhra Pradesh — long before online matchmaking became common. That trust is still at the center of everything we do today.

Maintained by Women

Our platform is run entirely by a dedicated team of women, working full-time to support every family through the process — from registration to finding the right match.

Recognition

Recipient of the Dasabda Mahila Award, presented by the Deputy Chief Minister for outstanding service at nominal fees.
